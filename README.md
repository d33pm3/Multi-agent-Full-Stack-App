SEBI Compliance Manager is an Agentic AI-powered RegTech platform for listed companies to manage SEBI LODR, PIT and governance compliance.
It replaces spreadsheets, emails and manual follow-ups with a central workspace for obligations, deadlines, risks, tasks, evidence, approvals and reporting. AI agents identify actions, flag gaps, assign ownership and escalate exceptions—under human oversight.
Built for Boards, Audit Committees, Company Secretaries, Chief Risk Officers and Compliance Officers to make compliance visible, accountable and audit-ready.
